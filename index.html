    Codenames

    How to play

    1. Split into a Red and a Blue team. Each team picks one spymaster.
    2. Put this phone in the middle. It shows 25 words. Only the spymasters know which words belong to which team.
    3. The spymasters open the site on their own phones, tap Join a game and enter the spymaster code to see the secret key.
    4. On your turn, your spymaster says one word and a number, like "Food, 2", meaning two words on the board link to "food".
    5. Your team taps words on this phone. Hit your own colour and keep guessing (up to the number + 1). Hit a neutral word or the other team's word and your turn ends.
    6. The black word is the assassin. Tap it and your team loses straight away. The first team to find all its words wins.
